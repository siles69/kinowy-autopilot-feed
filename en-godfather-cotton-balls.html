The Godfather — Brando's Cotton Ball Trick

Marlon Brando's iconic jowly look in The Godfather came from a trick with cotton balls. He stuffed small pieces of cotton inside his cheeks to give his face a heavier, aging appearance for the role. Director Francis Ford Coppola worried it would distort his voice too much to be usable. Brando insisted on keeping it, and the muffled, gravelly voice became part of the character's legend. That simple prop helped define one of the most imitated performances in film history.
