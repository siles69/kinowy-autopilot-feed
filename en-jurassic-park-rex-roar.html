Jurassic Park — The T-Rex Roar

The terrifying roar of the T-Rex in Jurassic Park isn't just one sound. Sound designers blended a baby elephant's squeal, a tiger's snarl, and an alligator's growl into a single layered roar. Combined with practical animatronics and groundbreaking CGI, the dinosaurs felt more real than anything audiences had seen before. The blend of old-school puppetry and new digital effects set a new standard for the entire industry. And it's still terrifying decades later.
