The Wizard of Oz — The Lion's Costume

The Cowardly Lion's costume in The Wizard of Oz was made from actual lion skins. Actor Bert Lahr had to wear the heavy pelt under hot studio lights for hours at a time. He reportedly lost several pounds of sweat during a single day of filming. What looked like comic relief on screen was one of the most physically brutal costumes in Hollywood history. And it helped make the Cowardly Lion one of cinema's most beloved characters.
