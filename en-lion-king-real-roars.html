The Lion King — Real Lions Recorded for the Roars

The roars in The Lion King weren't created by sound effects artists alone. Sound designers traveled to record real lions, tigers, and other big cats to capture authentic roars and growls. Those recordings were layered together to build the powerful voice of characters like Mufasa and Scar. The result sounded far more real than anything a studio could invent from scratch. It set a new bar for how animated animals should sound on screen.
