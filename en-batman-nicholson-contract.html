Jack Nicholson's Batman Contract Secretly Made Him Millions

When Jack Nicholson agreed to play the Joker in Tim Burton's Batman, his agents negotiated an unusual deal instead of a bigger upfront paycheck. Nicholson chose a smaller salary combined with a percentage of the film's box office and merchandising profits. At the time, nobody expected Batman merchandise, toys, and soundtracks to become such a massive worldwide phenomenon. Thanks to that gamble, Nicholson reportedly ended up earning somewhere between fifty and ninety million dollars from a single movie role.
