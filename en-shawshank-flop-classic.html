Shawshank Redemption Actually Flopped in Theaters Before Becoming a Classic

The Shawshank Redemption is now regularly ranked as one of the greatest films ever made, often topping fan-voted lists on sites like IMDb. When it was released in theaters in 1994, though, it was actually a box office flop, barely earning back its budget during its initial run. The film only found its massive audience later, through heavy rotation on cable television and word-of-mouth video rentals. It's a rare case of a movie failing completely at the box office and still becoming one of the most beloved films of all time.
