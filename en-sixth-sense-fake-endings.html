The Sixth Sense Cast Was Secretly Given Fake Endings to Hide the Twist

To protect the twist ending of The Sixth Sense, director M. Night Shyamalan didn't just keep the secret from audiences, he kept it from most of his own cast. Several actors were given entirely fake versions of the script's final pages, describing endings that never actually happen in the movie. This way, even if someone accidentally leaked details during filming, the real twist would stay hidden until opening night. The strategy worked so well that the ending became one of the most famous surprises in modern film history.
