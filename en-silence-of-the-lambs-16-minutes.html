Anthony Hopkins Actually Won an Oscar With Only 16 Minutes on Screen

Anthony Hopkins won the Academy Award for Best Actor for playing Hannibal Lecter in The Silence of the Lambs. What most people don't realize is that his total screen time in the film adds up to only about sixteen minutes. Despite barely appearing on camera, his performance was so unsettling and precise that the Academy gave him the Oscar for a leading role anyway. To this day, it remains one of the shortest performances ever to win Best Actor.
