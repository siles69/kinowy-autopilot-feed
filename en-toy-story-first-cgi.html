Toy Story — The First Fully Computer-Animated Film

Toy Story was the first feature film ever made entirely with computer animation. Pixar's computers were so limited at the time that a single frame could take hours to render. The studio ran its machines around the clock, every night, just to finish the movie on schedule. Many in Hollywood doubted a fully computer-animated film could even hold an audience's attention. Instead, it launched an entirely new era of animated filmmaking.
