Casablanca — The Ending Nobody Had Decided

The ending of Casablanca wasn't decided until shooting was almost finished. Writers kept revising the script scene by scene, and the cast genuinely didn't know how the love triangle would resolve. Ingrid Bergman later said she played her scenes without knowing which man her character truly loved. That uncertainty gave her performance a raw, unresolved tension the writers couldn't have planned. The film went on to become one of the most celebrated romances ever made.
