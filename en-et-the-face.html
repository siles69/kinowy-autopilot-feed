E.T. — The Face

The face of E.T. wasn't based on any single creature. Designers blended the features of a pug, a Nobel Prize-winning physicist, and an aging poet into one gentle, wrinkled face. Steven Spielberg wanted an alien that looked wise but harmless, something a child could love instantly. The mix of unlikely inspirations created one of the most iconic movie faces ever designed. And audiences fell for it instantly.
