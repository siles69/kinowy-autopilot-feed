Bill Murray Actually Improvised Most of His Lines in Ghostbusters

Many of Bill Murray's most quoted lines in Ghostbusters were never actually written in the original script. Director Ivan Reitman gave Murray and his co-stars room to improvise entire scenes on set, especially in the chemistry-heavy moments between the ghostbusting team. Editors would then piece together the funniest, most natural-sounding takes from hours of unscripted footage. This loose, improvisational approach became one of the biggest reasons the film's humor still feels so fresh decades later.
