Die Hard — Filmed in a Real, Unfinished Skyscraper

Die Hard was filmed inside a real skyscraper that was still under construction. The production used the mostly finished Fox Plaza building in Los Angeles, which hadn't been fully leased out yet. Empty, unfinished floors gave the filmmakers huge, realistic spaces to work with at a fraction of the cost of building sets. Some scenes even used areas still missing walls or flooring. That real, unfinished building gave the film a grounded scale that a studio set could never fully match.
