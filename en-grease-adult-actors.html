Grease — Grown Adults Playing Teenagers

The teenagers in Grease were played by actors well into their twenties and thirties. John Travolta was in his early twenties, but several of his co-stars were even older, some pushing thirty-five. Producers cast older actors on purpose, believing audiences wouldn't notice once the singing and dancing started. The gamble worked, and nobody seemed to mind the age gap at all. Grease became one of the highest-grossing musicals ever made.
