Jumanji Actually Used Real Wild Animals for That Stampede Scene

For the stampede scene in Jumanji, the filmmakers wanted the chaos of elephants, rhinos, and other animals rushing through a small town to feel completely real. Instead of relying only on the CGI technology available in 1995, the production actually brought real, trained wild animals onto the set alongside the actors. Combining practical animals with early computer effects made the sequence far more convincing than either method could achieve alone. It remains one of the most technically ambitious scenes of its era, mixing real fur and hooves with digital enhancement.
