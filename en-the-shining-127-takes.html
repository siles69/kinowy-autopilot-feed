The Shining — 127 Takes

One scene in The Shining needed 127 takes before Stanley Kubrick was satisfied. Actress Shelley Duvall had to swing a baseball bat at Jack Nicholson over and over, take after exhausting take. The repetition set a Guinness World Record for the most retakes of a single scene with dialogue. Duvall later said the process left her physically and emotionally drained. But it produced one of the most tense moments in horror movie history.
