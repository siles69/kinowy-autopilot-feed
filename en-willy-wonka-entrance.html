Gene Wilder Secretly Planned That Iconic Willy Wonka Entrance Himself

When Gene Wilder agreed to play Willy Wonka, he made one unusual demand before signing on. He insisted that his character's first entrance show him walking with a cane and a visible limp, then suddenly trip forward into a perfect somersault before popping up smiling. Wilder explained that from that moment on, no one would know whether to believe anything Wonka said or did. The crew and cast had no idea this was coming, so their startled reactions on camera were completely genuine.
