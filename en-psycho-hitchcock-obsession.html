Psycho — Hitchcock's Obsession

Alfred Hitchcock went to extreme lengths to protect the twist ending of Psycho. He bought up as many copies of the original novel as he could find to keep the secret from spreading. He also required theaters to stop admitting latecomers once the film had started, a policy almost unheard of at the time. Audiences had to see Psycho from the very beginning or not at all. That obsession with secrecy became part of the film's legendary marketing, and it helped make Psycho one of the most talked-about thrillers ever made.
