Alien — The Real Shock

The cast of Alien had no idea what was about to happen in the infamous chestburster scene. Director Ridley Scott kept the actors mostly in the dark about how graphic the effect would be. When the creature burst out in a spray of blood, the shocked reactions on screen were completely real. Actress Veronica Cartwright reportedly fainted from the shock of the take. That unscripted terror became one of the most famous scares in movie history.
