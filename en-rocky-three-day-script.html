Rocky — Written in Three Days

Sylvester Stallone wrote the script for Rocky in just three and a half days. Studios loved the story and offered him hundreds of thousands of dollars for the rights, but only if another actor played the lead. Stallone turned down the money every time and refused to sell unless he could star in it himself. He was virtually broke and reportedly down to his last few dollars when he finally got his way. The gamble paid off, and Rocky became one of the biggest underdog stories in film history.
