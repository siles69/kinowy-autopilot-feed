Home Alone — The Face Copied From a Painting

The most famous image from Home Alone wasn't invented by the filmmakers at all. Macaulay Culkin's hands-on-cheeks, wide-mouthed expression was directly inspired by Edvard Munch's painting The Scream. The crew showed him the artwork and asked him to recreate that exact pose for the poster and promotional stills. It became one of the most recognizable faces in movie history. Decades later, people still recreate that expression without knowing where it came from.
