The Exorcist — The Set Kept Freezing Cold

The set of The Exorcist was kept freezing cold on purpose during filming. Crews installed industrial refrigeration equipment to drop the temperature low enough that actors' breath would show on camera. Cast members worked for hours in near-freezing conditions, wearing minimal clothing for their scenes. The visible breath added an eerie, otherworldly detail the filmmakers couldn't have faked convincingly. It became one of many production details that made the film feel unsettlingly real.
