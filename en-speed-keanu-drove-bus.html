Keanu Reeves Actually Drove the Bus Himself in Speed

In Speed, Keanu Reeves plays a cop trying to keep a bus above fifty miles per hour or it will explode. For many of the driving scenes, Reeves wasn't just acting behind the wheel, he was actually driving the bus himself on closed-off Los Angeles freeways. The production even needed a special rig so a stunt driver could take over from underneath if something went wrong. Reeves reportedly enjoyed it so much that he asked to keep doing his own driving stunts throughout filming.
