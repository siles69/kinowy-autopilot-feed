The Terminator — Arnold Almost Played the Hero

Arnold Schwarzenegger almost played the hero in The Terminator, not the villain. Director James Cameron originally wanted him for the role of Kyle Reese, the human protector sent back in time. During a lunch meeting, Schwarzenegger kept describing how the Terminator itself should look and move. Cameron realized he was talking to the perfect killing machine, not the hero. That casting switch created one of cinema's most iconic villains.
