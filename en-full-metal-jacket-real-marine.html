The Drill Instructor in Full Metal Jacket Was Actually a Real Marine

For Full Metal Jacket, Stanley Kubrick originally hired R. Lee Ermey only as a technical advisor, since Ermey was a real former Marine Corps drill instructor. During rehearsals, Ermey started improvising insults and commands straight from his actual military training, faster and harsher than anything in the script. Kubrick was so impressed that he fired the actor originally cast for the role and gave the part to Ermey instead. Much of Ermey's now-famous dialogue in the film was never written down, it came directly from his real career barking at recruits.
