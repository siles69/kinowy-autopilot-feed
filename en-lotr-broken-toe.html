Viggo Mortensen Actually Broke His Toe Filming Lord of the Rings

When Viggo Mortensen's character discovers his friends have been captured in The Fellowship of the Ring, he was supposed to simply scream in frustration and kick a nearby helmet. Nobody warned him the prop was solid metal, not the lightweight replica he expected. Mortensen actually broke two toes on impact, and the raw pain on his face was completely real, not acted. Director Peter Jackson loved the reaction so much that he kept it in the final film, toes and all.
