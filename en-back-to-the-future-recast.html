Back to the Future — Five Weeks With a Different Actor

Back to the Future was five weeks into filming with a completely different actor as Marty McFly. Eric Stoltz played the role for weeks before producers decided his performance wasn't landing the comedy. They made the costly decision to recast and reshoot almost everything from scratch. Michael J. Fox stepped in, filming his network sitcom by day and the movie by night. That last-minute swap turned into one of the most beloved performances in movie history.
